Terms and Conditions

These terms describe responsible use of NearServe, a local service booking platform for customers and workers.

Platform Purpose

NearServe connects customers and local service workers such as electricians, plumbers, and carpenters.

Worker Responsibilities

Workers must provide correct information, valid contact details, accurate service details, and truthful verification documents.

Customer Responsibilities

Customers must use the platform responsibly, provide correct booking information, communicate respectfully, and avoid fake or harmful requests.

Booking, Payment, and Ratings

NearServe includes booking requests, a ₹29 online booking payment flow, job status updates, and a rating flow after work completion.

Worker Verification

NearServe may verify workers before giving full platform access. Admin review may be required for worker documents and account status.

Misuse and Restrictions

Misuse, fake documents, fake bookings, harmful activity, or attempts to bypass platform rules may lead to account restriction.
