Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NearServe handles user, booking, and worker verification information on https://nearserve-connect.web.app.

Information We Collect

NearServe collects only required user details for booking, account access, communication, and worker verification.

Worker Verification Documents

Worker documents are used only for worker identity verification. Documents are reviewed securely by the admin and are not shown publicly.

How Data Is Used

  • To manage customer bookings and worker requests.
  • To support chat, booking status, payment, and rating flows.
  • To verify workers before giving access to platform features.

Data Sharing

User data is not sold. Verification documents are not displayed publicly or shared for marketing purposes.

Deletion Requests

Users can request deletion of their account or uploaded verification document by contacting cloudrashmithas132@gmail.com.
